How it works

This app lets you create, edit, and store Magic: The Gathering decks. Pages now follow that job instead of a generic Home / Log In / About bar.

  1. Welcome — what the vault is, and the three steps.
  2. Create account / Sign in — unlock your local vault.
  3. Builder — preview, search, copy counts, spells and lands.
  4. Vault — save and reopen lists at the bottom of the builder.
