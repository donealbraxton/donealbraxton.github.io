What was here, and what changed

In 2022 this was a GitHub Pages senior project: Bootstrap Cover, gray gradient, and four flat HTML files. The product idea is the same. The chrome is new.

Kept

Home hero and Get Started, two Scryfall cards, About, sign up / sign in, search-add-save decks.

Changed

Vite src/ layout, shared header, no Bootstrap CSS, five-color playmat palette, Fraunces + Outfit type, clearer page names.
